# Steakbite

## About

A 1v1 local coop strategy game where you're forced to eat steaks. Avoid the poisoned one and poison your friend to survive!
Inspired by Buckshot Roulette, Russian Roulette, and steaks, this game was made for the University of San Diego's Video Game Design Club's Triton Game Development Challenge (yes, it's the UCSD VGDC TGDC).

## How to Play

Use the mouse or the arrow keys and space/enter to navigate the UI.

> ![NOTE] Spotlight
> If the spotlight disappears press tab or use a mouse to get it back.

The rules are as follows:

- There are 20 steaks, 8 of which are poisoned (might be changed later for balancing)
- Two steaks eaten and you are dead.
- Per steak eaten, you get a point to spend on powerups before you next eat a steak.
- Use your powerups and luck to outlast your opponent and entertain the butler!
